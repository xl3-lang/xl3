# Privacy

xl3.io is the documentation site for an open-source project. There are no
accounts, no logins, and no forms — nothing you type is collected or sent
anywhere.

## What we measure

We use [PostHog](https://posthog.com) to see which documentation pages get
read, so we know what to write more of. What is recorded depends on the choice
you made in the consent notice:

| | **Accept** | **Decline** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stored on your device | A first-party cookie identifying your browser | Only a note that you declined, so you are not asked again |
| Page URL, referrer, language | Recorded | Recorded |
| Browser and operating system | Recorded | Recorded |
| Approximate country | Recorded | Not available |
| Recognised across visits | Yes | No — a fresh identifier each day |

Declining does not turn measurement off. It switches PostHog to a mode where
your visit is counted using an identifier derived on PostHog's servers from
your IP address and browser, salted with a random value that is discarded
every day. Nothing that identifies you is written to your device, and the
identifier cannot be traced back to you or reconnected to yesterday's.

We do not record sessions, we do not run surveys, and we never build a profile
of an individual visitor. There is no advertising on this site and no
third-party tracker other than PostHog — no external fonts, scripts, or CDN
assets are loaded.

Files you open in the [browser converter](/try) are processed entirely inside
your browser. They are never uploaded.

## Who processes it

PostHog acts as our data processor on **PostHog Cloud EU**, hosted in
Frankfurt, Germany. Analytics data stays within the EU and is not transferred
elsewhere. IP addresses are not stored.

## Legal basis

If you accepted, the basis is your consent — Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, together
with Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive for the cookie itself.

If you declined, the only thing kept on your device is the record of that
refusal, which is strictly necessary to honour it and therefore needs no
consent of its own under Article 5(3). The remaining processing is limited to
aggregate audience measurement and rests on our legitimate interest in
understanding which documentation is useful — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time. This clears the stored choice and brings
the consent notice back:

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If your browser
sends a Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a decline and never ask.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes request logs, including
IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse — see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Contact

at [github.com/xl3-lang/xl3/issues](https://github.com/xl3-lang/xl3/issues) or
sent to `privacy@xl3.io`.
